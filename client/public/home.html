Privacy is foundational to humanity and democracy.

The Why

Without a change, surveillance capitalism will continue to railroad the human experience as the “raw material for the hidden commercial practices of extraction, prediction, and sales.”1

MoreLess about The Why

Yet AI is also a highly productive tool, which is why our access must be divorced from the surveillance systems with which they are currently bundled.

Whether it’s the votes we cast, the products we buy, or just the subconscious decisions we make while viewing commercially-controlled content, at least one thing is certain: without privacy, our future is in the hands of the platforms controlling what we see, don’t see, and everything in between.2

The What

A privacy-first AI platform (think ChatGPT-interface but with more models to choose from) which democratizes enterprise-grade privacy, giving individual users a level of control previously only available to organizations managing their own AI infrastructure.

MoreLess about The What

In addition to each user starting from a place of anonymity (no user info is included in the sessions sent to the AI and search providers), additional controls let you determine if and how your data can be used. Starting with the most restrictive, these include:

  1. 01 Zero Data Retention (ZDR) Most restrictive
  2. 02 No training on user data
  3. 03 No restrictions Still anonymous

The How

Enterprise-grade privacy for AI is well established with platforms like LibreChat, which is trusted by thousands of organizations worldwide including Shopify, Daimler Truck and Boston University.

MoreLess about The How

This project is a fork of LibreChat with additional customizations for privacy, model selectability, and subscriptions. However, the ‘how’ is likely to change, as the pace of change in this industry makes most documentation obsolete by the time it’s published. For instance, since starting this project, Stripe (the payment processor used here) has entered into an agreement to acquire OpenRouter (the LLM model selector used here) and LibreChat has been acquired by ClickHouse. These changes will either simplify or complicate this project and only time will tell. The ‘how’ that matters most is where there’s a will, there’s a way. Accordingly, an alternative platform using none of the above mentioned is also in development. In the big picture, making things more private is simpler than managing surveillance systems, so the path to greater privacy is always there once we decide to take it.
